## Not used, but worth knowing
- **Passenger count:** sources disagree (36 passengers, or 37 including Cooper). The script avoids the number.
- **Seat:** 18C or 18E, depending on the source. Not used.
- **Barbara Dayton and other suspects:** left out to keep the line-up to five.
- **2024 claims** of a McCoy parachute (Dan Gryder) and the 2026 "one-in-a-billion parachute" coverage: recent and unresolved. Could become a "latest developments" note in the description.
