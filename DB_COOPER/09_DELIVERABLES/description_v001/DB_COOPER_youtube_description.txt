On 24 November 1971, a passenger calling himself "Dan Cooper" hijacked Northwest Orient Flight 305, a Boeing 727 from Portland to Seattle. That night he jumped from the plane's rear stairs with $200,000, and he was never found.

55 years later, the FBI has looked at more than 800 suspects, a boy has found $5,800 of the ransom in the sand of the Columbia River, and the case is still the only unsolved hijacking in American commercial aviation.

In this animated documentary we lay out the clues, the suspects and the three possible endings, and let you decide.
