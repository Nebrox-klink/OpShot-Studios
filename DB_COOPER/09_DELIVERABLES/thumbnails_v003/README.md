**What the winners share:**
1. **The name, or the iconic image of the case.** Most show the FBI composite sketch (with sunglasses) or "D.B. COOPER" in the text.
2. **Heavy condensed type**, usually white with a thick black outline. Some put black text on a white or yellow bar.
3. **One phrase on a solid highlight box** (red or yellow), plus a small red label.
4. **A red arrow or circle on the clue:** the wreckage, the dig site, the evidence.
5. **A punchy, high-contrast picture** with darker edges.
6. Many make false "finally solved / DNA confirms / identity revealed" claims in fake news-banner layouts. **We do not copy that.** It breaks our facts rule, and the case is unsolved.

## The v003 style
- **Type:** Anton (SIL Open Font License; the font and its licence are in `../tools/fonts/`), white with a thick black outline and a hard shadow.
- **Colour:**
  - one key phrase in **black on a yellow box** (the strongest contrast; it replaces the amber that "was not working");
  - a **red tag** for "THE D.B. COOPER CASE" or "UNSOLVED SINCE 1971";
  - a **red arrow and ring** on the clue.
- **Picture:** contrast up 28%, saturation up 22%, sharpened, with a dark vignette and a soft dark patch behind the text.
- **Still our rules:** no faces, no fake news banners. Every claim is from the film:
  - he jumped from the rear door;
  - $5,800 was found in the sand;
  - unsolved since 1971;
  - he was never found.
