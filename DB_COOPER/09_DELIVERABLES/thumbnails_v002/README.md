# D.B. Cooper thumbnails v002 (1280×720 JPEG, each under 2 MB)

Revised after checking v001 against the producer's thumbnail guide (`thumbnail.md`, 23 Nov). These are its "Best Practices" checks: readable at about 100 px, 3–7 words, white text with a dark stroke and shadow, at most 3 colours, and a grayscale test.

**Changes from v001:**
- **Kicker removed** on T1–T4. "THE D.B. COOPER CASE" and the date could not be read at mobile size and only added noise. T5 (the title card) keeps it.
- **Black outline on all headline text**, plus the existing shadow.
- **T2 shortened** to "HE JUMPED. / $200,000. / NEVER FOUND.", so the text is larger.
- **T3 split** into "$5,800 / IN THE SAND. / WHERE'S / THE REST?", with "THE REST?" in amber, so the question can be read on mobile. Before this, amber on light sand failed the grayscale test.

**Not taken from the guide:**
- Faces: the film never shows a face. This is a production rule.
- "Predicted CTR" numbers: they are not measured. Use YouTube Test & Compare instead.

`mobile_and_grayscale_check.jpg` shows all five at 168×94 (YouTube's mobile size, enlarged 3×), in colour above and in grayscale below.

**Recommendation:** test T2 against T1 in Test & Compare. T3 is the backup. The title pairings are the same as in `../thumbnails_v001/README.md`.
