# D.B. Cooper: Kling motion pack v001 (8 clips, round 1)

Make these by hand on kling.ai, then upload the results here. I check every clip frame by frame and cut it into the animatic.

## Settings for every clip
- **Mode:** Image to Video. Use the JPG with the same name as the start frame.
- **Length:** 5 s. **Aspect ratio:** 16:9.
- **Model and quality:** the cheapest model that looks good. Standard mode or 720p is fine for a test; the edit runs at 1080p, and 720p scales up acceptably, as it did on NOKAS.
- **Sound:** off or none (we add our own).
- **Takes:** 1 per clip (at most two attempts per shot).
- **Creativity/relevance slider, if shown:** lean toward following the image, about 0.6–0.7.
- **Negative prompt (paste in every clip):**
  > text, letters, words, numbers, logos, signs, subtitles, watermark, faces, face turning toward camera, people walking, extra people, hands moving, morphing, warping, style change, photorealistic, 3D render, camera shake, fast motion, flicker

**Name the downloads** `K01.mp4`, `K02.mp4` and so on, then upload them in this chat.

**Our rules for every clip:**
- no lip sync;
- no hands doing anything precise;
- no crowds in motion;
- no text appearing;
- figures never turn toward the camera.

If a clip breaks one of these, we keep the still with a free push-in instead.
