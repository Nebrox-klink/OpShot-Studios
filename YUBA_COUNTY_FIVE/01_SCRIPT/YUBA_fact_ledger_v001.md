# THE YUBA COUNTY FIVE: fact ledger v001

Checked on 24 November 2026 against `YUBA_preproduction_v001.md`; rows S15–S20 added for v002.

**Status key:**
- ✅ confirmed by at least two independent sources;
- ⚠ found in one source chain only; check before the voice is recorded;
- 🔶 contested or reported differently (the film presents it as uncertain);
- ⛔ not used in the film.

**Note on sources.** The main 1978 reporting is Cynthia Gorney, *"5 'Boys' Who Never Came Back"*, The Washington Post, 6 July 1978, and Dave Smollar / Cathleen Decker in the Los Angeles Times (10 March and 19 June 1978). Those pages are blocked from this environment (washingtonpost.com, newspapers.com, web.archive.org), so they were **not read directly**. Their content is taken from the English Wikipedia article "Yuba County Five" (read 24 Nov 2026), which cites them line by line, and cross-checked against later outlets found by search: Mental Floss, All That's Interesting, Grunge, Historic Mysteries, ABC10 / KCRA anniversary coverage. Fan sites, podcasts and forums are not used as sources.
**Before recording:** the producer (or a session with access) should read the Washington Post article once and confirm the ⚠ rows.
