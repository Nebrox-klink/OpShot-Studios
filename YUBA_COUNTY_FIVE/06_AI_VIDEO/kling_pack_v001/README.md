# Yuba County Five: Kling pack v001 (hero clips for the producer's own Kling plan)

Six hero clips, each made from its start frame in `start_frames/`. Settings: image-to-video, 16:9, 5 s, **sound off**, the best quality your credits allow. Upload the finished clips here as `K_<ID>.mp4` (e.g. `K_401.mp4`).

**Same rules as always:** no faces, no lip sync, no hands doing precise work, no crowds moving, no text. If a clip shows a face or warps the car, it goes back (at most two tries per shot).

Add this to the end of every prompt:
> *Keep the painterly gouache illustration style exactly, visible brush strokes, no photorealism. Nobody turns toward the camera; no faces. No text. Smooth, slow, cinematic.*
