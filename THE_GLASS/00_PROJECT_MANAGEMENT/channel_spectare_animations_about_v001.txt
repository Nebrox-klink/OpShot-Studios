=== CHANNEL DESCRIPTION (About section; under 1,000 characters) ===

Spectare: Latin for "to watch, to observe, to look closely".

Spectare Animations makes animated documentaries about real events: the crimes, disasters and turning points that changed people's lives, told carefully and with respect for those who were there.

Every film starts with months of research in news archives, court records and interviews. The stories are then rebuilt as painterly animation, because some moments can't be filmed, and some people deserve not to be shown.

No glorification. No invented facts. Just a close look at what happened, and what it cost.

I make every video on my own: research, script, visuals, sound and editing. If you enjoy the films, subscribing and liking genuinely helps the channel grow and lets me keep making them.

New documentaries when they're ready, not before.
