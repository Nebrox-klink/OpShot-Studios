=== CHANNEL DESCRIPTION (About section; under 1,000 characters) ===

Spectare: Latin for "to watch, to observe, to look closely".

Spectare Animations makes animated documentaries about history's great mysteries: unsolved cases, vanished people, strange events and puzzles that still don't have an answer.

Each film lays out the evidence like a case file. What actually happened? What do the clues say? Which theories hold up, and which fall apart? You get the facts, the theories and the questions, and you decide for yourself.

Every story is researched from archives, investigations and expert sources, then brought to life in painterly hand-crafted animation.

Exciting to watch. Worth knowing afterwards.

I make every video on my own: research, script, visuals, sound and editing. If you enjoy the films, liking and subscribing genuinely helps the channel grow and lets me keep making them.

New mysteries when they're ready, not before.
