## A · What changes because of this research

Three discoveries go to the heart of the story, and none of them is in our scripts:

1. **The robbery was a funeral.** Toska's best friend and co-planner, **William Ellingsen**, was killed by a stray bullet in Oslo on 6 February 2004, four days after their first attempt failed. Toska gave the eulogy. Afterwards he made the robbery bigger, "spectacular and massive", to honour William. On 5 April the robbers wore **white mourning armbands** taped to their overalls (F06, F07, F15). One participant said the grief "led to irrational thinking and planning."
2. **The vault was open.** After all the battering, the barred gate to the main vault, where the hundreds of millions were, **was not locked**. They could have reached in and lifted a handle. They expected 300–400 million and left with 57.4, mostly in 200-kroner notes (F27, F28). Meanwhile **three women employees wheeled 60 million kroner into a lift** while the glass was being shot (F24).
3. **Toska was caught during Klungland's memorial.** On 5 April 2005, at Norwegian police request, the British email provider slowed logins across Europe. Toska stayed online longer than usual, **reading news about the anniversary memorial for Klungland**. He was arrested in Málaga while that memorial was under way in Stavanger, and the news spread through the gathering as a murmur (F48, F49).

Plus a fact about Klungland that gives the story a quiet, documented echo: he also **drove a bus**, on church trips for pensioners (F35). On 5 April, a bus blocked his escape (F33).

## D · Open questions and conflicting claims

1. **Why the February attempt was called off:** money being moved (NRK) or radio failure (Dødsranet). The book should settle it.
2. **Number of shots:** 113 (police count, Aftenblad/NRK) vs "about 120" (no.wiki) vs "at least 100" (Schumann). Use **113**.
3. **Where Klungland died:** Kongsgata by the Maritim hotel (Wikipedia) or Klubbgata (NRK). Probably the corner of the two streets; confirm with the judgment or the plaque location.
4. **The wound:** head (most sources) vs throat (Klikk). It isn't needed: the script shows no wound.
5. **Where the cars burned:** Sørmarka, Ullandhaug or Smedheia. They're adjacent; use "the woods at Sørmarka".
6. **Toska's confession:** via his lawyer on 12 April 2005 (our v005 source) or in court on 22 September 2005 (no.wiki). Possibly both.
7. **Cost to the state:** over 200 million or over 300 million kroner.
8. **The 15 March attempt:** a single source (Dødsranet via Klikk).
9. **Unreachable primary sources:**
   - The Supreme Court judgment HR-2007-1184-A (Lovdata Pro needs a subscription; the archived free PDF is on web.archive.org, which the environment change didn't allow).
   - The district-court judgment of 10 March 2006.
   - Weather data for 5 April 2004 (MET Norway needs a free login). **The weather in our script is invented until this is checked.**

## E · The most dramatic documented details most retellings leave out

1. White mourning armbands for a dead friend (F15).
2. The unlocked vault gate (F28).
3. Three women saving 60 million kroner in a lift while the glass was being shot (F24).
4. Toska budgeted 10–15 seconds for the glass, then ran around with a watch in his hand (F20, F29).
5. Toska was arrested while reading about Klungland's memorial, during that memorial (F48, F49).
6. Klungland himself delivered the robbery warning at that morning's briefing (F12).
7. Klungland drove buses for pensioners, and a bus blocked his reversing (F33, F35).
8. A single lost earplug carried Toska's DNA (F39).
9. The only photograph of the robbery was taken by a tourist from a hotel (F40).
10. Officer Håland, a revolver with 12 rounds against AG3 rifles; afterwards he confronted the police chief, furious that the warning had been held back for two weeks (F25; R04).

## F · Sensitivity and legal notes for Phase 2

- **Klungland's son Kjetil** (his account of that day, his father in the vehicle, the family's forgiveness) is on record with NRK. Using it, especially the image of his father in the car, requires **contacting the family first**. The vehicle image should never be shown.
- **The three women employees and the NOKAS staff:** 32 had psychological late effects ten years later (F57). No names, no faces, no dramatised panic.
- **Living convicted persons:** Toska declined NRK interviews; Schumann speaks openly. Keep invented dialogue for real people to a minimum and mark it. Present Schumann's "mistake" claim only as his claim, beside the court's finding (F34).
- **The NOKAS guard (F02)** is unnamed in the sources. Keep him unnamed.
- **The Ellingsen angle** explains Toska's escalation but must not turn the robbery into a tribute. Frame it as grief driving a reckless decision, paid for by others.
