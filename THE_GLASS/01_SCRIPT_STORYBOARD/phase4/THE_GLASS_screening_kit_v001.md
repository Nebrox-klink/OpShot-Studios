# THE GLASS: screening kit (v001)

**File to play:** `09_DELIVERABLES/animatic_v008/THE_GLASS_animatic_audio_v008b_bm_george.mp4` (13:11).
**Who:** 3–5 people who haven't read the script. At least 3 should not know the NOKAS case; ideally 1 Norwegian who does.

## Before

- Watch it alone, or one person at a time, so their answers don't influence each other.
- Ask for consent: "Can I take notes on your answers? They're only used to improve the film."
- Say only this: **"It's a 13-minute documentary, an audio draft. The pictures aren't made yet. Just listen."** Nothing about the case, the victim or the theme.

## During

- Play it once, without pausing.
- Write down the timestamp whenever they look away, check a phone or fidget (the player's clock is fine), and any visible reaction (a wince, a laugh, a "huh?").

## After: ask in this order, word for word, without prompting

1. In one sentence, what was it about?
2. Who did you care about most?
3. Was there any moment you wanted the robbers to succeed?
4. Where were you confused? Any names, times or places?
5. Where did you lose interest?
6. How did the line "It would become a monument. Just not to William" land?
7. Did anything feel too neat, or too set up?
8. What did the last line mean to you?
9. What do you remember about the vault gate?
10. Was anything disrespectful?

**Don't ask about the bus.** If someone mentions it on their own, note it word for word.
