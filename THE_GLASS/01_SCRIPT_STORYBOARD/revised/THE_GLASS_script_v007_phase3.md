# THE GLASS: script v007 (Phase 3, written for the ear)

**Status:** for approval before Phase 4 (review gates). Nothing generated.
**Built on:** Phase 2 story design v001 (approved) and `02_RESEARCH/THE_GLASS_fact_ledger_v002.csv`.
**Change from Phase 2:** the four Blender 3D sequences are dropped. Their beats (scenes 6, 14, 18 and 23) are now 2D still-based shots.

## How to read this script

- Every narration line ends with its source tag:
  - `[F##]`: a fact in the ledger.
  - `[FRAMING]`: the narrator's interpretation, not a factual claim.
  - `[DRAMATISED]`: an invented detail that changes no core fact.
  - `[CONTEXT]`: general background.
  - Medium-confidence facts carry `(M)`.
- The tags are stripped from the version Atlas reads (`narration_v007.tsv`).
- `*(beat)*` means a pause of about one second, and `*(hold)*` about three. Atlas pauses come from paragraph breaks and SSML `<break>` tags later.
- **Shot tiers** (from the v006 quality strategy):
  - **A:** an art-directed still with a camera move or parallax, plus overlays made in the edit.
  - **B:** image-to-video, 5 s, subtle motion only.
  - **C:** not used in this script.
- Hard limits apply to every shot:
  - no lip sync;
  - no hands doing precise work;
  - no crowds in motion;
  - no glass breaking in motion;
  - no fire spreading;
  - no text inside images (all text, numbers, maps and clocks are made in the edit).
- **People on screen:**
  - Robbers are faceless silhouettes.
  - Toska is seen from behind or in shadow.
  - Klungland appears only as a respectful painted portrait (made from a reference, approved separately), never in the vehicle.
  - NOKAS staff are hands, backs and objects only.
- **Dialogue:** the film has no dramatised dialogue. Every quoted line is a documented on-record quote, read by the narrator. No words are invented for anyone.
- **Timing:** Atlas reads at about 14 characters per second. The per-scene timing table is in section 3.
