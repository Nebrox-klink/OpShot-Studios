# THE GLASS: narration v005 (rewrite)

**Why:** in rough cut v003 the narrator spoke for only about 49% of the 13 minutes, so many shots sat in silence. v005 fills the 2D shots to roughly 80%. It adds short lines to the 3D scenes and keeps the memorial (3D 3) almost silent. It also makes David Toska's role as the planner much more concrete.

**Length:** about 8,600 characters, up from 6,160. The estimated narration time is about 9.5 of the 13 minutes. Every line is sized to its shot (Verdict reads about 15–17 characters per second).

**Tone rules kept:**
- Toska is shown as calculating and methodical, but his cleverness always comes with its cost: the DNA mistake, the failed eight-minute plan and Klungland's death. He is never made heroic.
- Klungland is treated with dignity, with no wound detail. The planned line about the head wound was softened to "One of the bullets struck him."

**Fact corrections from the new sources:**
- "spent a year" is now "planned for months". Toska began planning in November 2003, according to English Wikipedia.
- "The plan took more than a year" was removed.
- The unrecovered amount is now 51 million kroner, per English Wikipedia, replacing "6 million accounted for, roughly 50 million".
- The arrest date is confirmed as 5 April 2005; both Wikipedias agree.
- Toska fired at the window first, then called Schumann (timeline at 08:03).

**Source key:**
- **W-EN:** en.wikipedia.org, *NOKAS robbery* and *David Toska* (read 17 Nov 2026).
- **W-NO:** no.wikipedia.org, *NOKAS-ranet* and *David Toska* (read 17 Nov 2026).
- **source_log:** the earlier verified sources in `00_PROJECT_MANAGEMENT/source_log.csv`.

**Still open:** the February 2004 courtyard episode rests only on source_log S04/S05 (secondary sources). Check it against court material before publishing.
