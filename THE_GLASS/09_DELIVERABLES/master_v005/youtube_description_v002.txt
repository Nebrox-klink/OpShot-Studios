At 08:02 on Monday 5 April 2004, a group of armed men stormed the NOKAS cash depot in Stavanger, Norway. They had planned for months and expected the window to break in fifteen seconds. It didn't.

The NOKAS robbery became one of the most notorious crimes in Norway's recent history. Police officer Arne Sigve Klungland was killed on the cathedral square. He was 53.

This animated documentary reconstructs what happened before, during and after that morning: the plan, the mistakes, the manhunt that followed, and the cost that never appeared in anyone's calculations.

In memory of Arne Sigve Klungland (1950 – 2004).
