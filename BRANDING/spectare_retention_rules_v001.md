# Spectare: retention rules v001 (24 Nov 2026)

**Producer's strategy:** viewers need to be stimulated almost all the time, so our animated films must keep giving the eye and ear something new, or people click away.
These rules apply to every film from THE YUBA COUNTY FIVE onward. The channel rules still hold: no faces, no lip sync, no hand work in motion, no crowds in motion, no text inside generated images (text belongs in the edit), facts only from the ledger.

## Where we are
D.B. Cooper: 65 shots in 9:53, an **average of 9.1 seconds per shot**, with 8 moving clips. That is slow for YouTube animation.

## The rules

| # | Rule | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Short shots.** No picture stays on screen unchanged for long. | Average **3–4 s** per cut; nothing over 6 s unless it is a designed pause |
| 2 | **Every frame moves.** No plain stills: either a Kling clip or local motion (push/pan, weather, light flicker, smoke, dust, water). | 100% of shots |
| 3 | **Something new every 5–8 s:** a cut, a new angle, an on-screen graphic, a sound change or a music change. | No 8 s stretch without a change |
| 4 | **Hook in the first 30 s:** the strangest fact first, then the question the film will answer. | The question by 0:20 |
| 5 | **Open loops:** each chapter ends with a question or "remember this", answered later. | One per chapter |
| 6 | **Motion graphics in the edit:** a map with the route drawing itself, red string snapping between pins, distance and time counters ("70 MILES"), clue cards, typewriter titles, highlighted circles on details. | At least one per 30 s |
| 7 | **One still, several shots:** each 2k image gives a wide, a medium and a close-up (punch-ins and pans on the same image), so the cut rate goes up without extra generation cost. | 2–3 cuts per still |
| 8 | **Sound never sits still:** effects on every cut and graphic (whoosh, paper, pin, typewriter), ambience changes per location, music that changes with each chapter. | Every cut has a sound |
| 9 | **Contrast, not chaos:** short, designed silences (1–2 s) before the biggest reveals. The drop is itself a stimulus. | 3–5 per film |
| 10 | **Chapters every 60–90 s:** clue cards reset attention and make good YouTube chapters. | 8–10 per film |

## What this means for THE YUBA COUNTY FIVE (10 min)
- **About 150–170 cuts** (instead of 65).
- **About 70 new stills** (~140 Higgsfield credits); each used for 2–3 cuts.
- **30–40 Kling clips** for movement inside the picture: walking figures from behind, the car on the road, headlights, snow falling off branches, the trailer door, meltwater.
- **Local motion** (`YUBA_COUNTY_FIVE/09_DELIVERABLES/tools/motion_fx.py`, free) on everything else.
- **Motion graphics** made locally: the route map, string, counters, clue cards.
- **Check after publishing:** YouTube Studio's audience-retention graph for each film. Every dip shows a place where viewers left, and that tells us which rule to tighten.
